BEHIND THE PRODUCT

How BazarBondhu works

  1. Understand your finances. Enter income, rent, utilities, savings and other household expenses.
  2. Calculate affordability. Grocery allowance = max(0, income − fixed expenses − savings goal).
  3. Plan with constraints. An explainable, bounded allocation model adjusts shopping quantities to a mathematical spending target.
  4. Answer questions locally. A small TF-IDF character n-gram model matches English/Bangla questions to supported intents.
  5. Make the result clear. Responses use calculated figures rather than made-up prices.
RESPONSIBLE INNOVATION

Ethics, privacy and limitations

01 / Nutritional limitations

The planner cannot determine whether a proposed quantity is healthy or adequate. It is not dietary guidance.

02 / Price accuracy

All starting prices are invented examples. Any dated observations are entered by users, retained locally and not independently verified by this app.

03 / Financial privacy

No account, tracker, remote API, or background upload. Inputs stay in memory until the page is refreshed or closed.

04 / Limited language model

The assistant recognizes a limited set of question types; it can misclassify ambiguous or unfamiliar questions and is not a general-purpose chatbot.
