Privacy

Only public-approved academic information is included in this portfolio.

Public information only

The portfolio is designed to present research, education, conference activity, scholarly service, public datasets, verified professional development and public scholarly profiles.

Intentionally excluded

Confidential manuscripts, reviewer reports, editorial correspondence, submission or manuscript IDs, private phone numbers, private files and non-public collaborator information are intentionally excluded.

Research confidentiality

Research that is not approved for public disclosure is not stored as a hidden website record. Aggregate research counts may be reported without exposing confidential details.

Contact enquiries and email delivery

The academic enquiry form collects the details that you choose to submit: your name, email address, enquiry topic and message. These details are transmitted to FormSubmit, an external email forwarding service, for delivery to the portfolio owner. This website has no submission database. FormSubmit states that submissions can be retained for up to 30 days. Do not include confidential manuscripts, patient records or other sensitive information in the form.

The separate “Compose email” links open your configured email application, where messages are handled by the sender’s and recipient’s email providers rather than the website contact form.

Visitor analytics

This portfolio uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure aggregate website usage and performance, including page views, visits, popular pages, referring websites, country-level traffic, and page loading metrics. Its JavaScript beacon sends limited usage and performance measurements to Cloudflare, an external service. According to Cloudflare, Web Analytics does not place cookies or use browser storage to track people, and does not identify individual visitors. Read about Cloudflare Web Analytics.

Analytics reports are for the portfolio owner and are not published as public visitor counters. Some browsers and privacy tools can block analytics collection, so reported totals may differ from all actual visits. The portfolio remains hosted on GitHub Pages.

Ask Razu AI

The portfolio assistant is restricted to public-approved portfolio records and is not intended to reveal private research or personal information.
