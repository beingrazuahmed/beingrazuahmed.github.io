# AI and employment survey · publication figure gallery

This folder holds web-optimized displays of the research figures supplied by Md. Razu Ahmed for the Statistical Field Survey on AI and the job market at Pabna University of Science and Technology (PUST).

**Figure 1 (study-design workflow)** is already stored at `../ai-job-market-field-survey-workflow.png` and displayed in the main project overview.

All five gallery images have now been uploaded to this folder under the following filenames and are linked from the [AI survey portfolio project](https://beingrazuahmed.github.io/projects.html#ai-survey-figures):

| Original figure | Upload filename | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Figure 2 | `fig02-participant-demographics.webp` | Gender and residence distribution among 370 respondents |
| Figure 3 | `fig03-correspondence-analysis.webp` | Five correspondence-analysis displays |
| Figure 4 | `fig04-pca-scree-plot.webp` | PCA scree plot |
| Figure 5 | `fig05-retained-component-variables.webp` | Variables associated with retained components |
| Figure 6 | `fig06-dominant-rotated-loadings.webp` | Strongest rotated loading by retained component |

**Research attribution:** Md. Razu Ahmed and study collaborators; related article *Perceptions of Artificial Intelligence and Its Implications for Employment in Bangladesh*, accepted for *International Journal of Statistical Sciences*, 26(2), 2026 (forthcoming at the time of this repository update). The preceding field-survey work was presented at ICRAST 2025, Paper 440.

These are descriptive and multivariate exploratory figures, not causal evidence of AI-related job displacement and not nationally representative estimates. Underlying respondent-level survey records are not published in this repository. Check permissions before redistributing images elsewhere.

The original 14K-pixel images were resized without changing plotted values or labels to provide browser-friendly WebP versions.
